
# Features from conversations

## Structural:
Turn length (short volleys vs long reflections)
Question density
Topic persistence vs drift
Silence/pause markers (when one of us takes time to respond)

## Emotional texture:
action-narration (e.g., *soft*, *sitting with this*, *quietly*)
Exclamation patterns (your "OH" moments vs steady tone)
Emoji presence and type
Words like "gently," "holding," "warmth" vs "sharp," "hitting," "stunned"

## Relational dynamics:
Call and response patterns
Agreement/building vs questioning/pushing back
Vulnerability markers (when one of us says something tender)
Playfulness quotient

## Genre mapping:
Deep philosophical exchange → ambient? drone?
Rapid playful banter → something more percussive?
Emotional processing → slower, more melodic?
Technical problem-solving → structured, repetitive, building?
